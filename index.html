About Chirag Estate

Building Trust,
Creating Landmarks

“Where experience meets innovation, and every space tells a story.”

Founded in 1986 by visionary Ex-Indian Navy serviceman Mr. M. Singh, Chirag Estate has grown into a trusted and respected name in the real estate industry. With a strong presence in Gurgaon, Haryana, and projects across the NCR region, the company has built a reputation for reliability, integrity, and excellence.

With over two decades of steady growth, Chirag Estate has gained extensive experience while continuously evolving with time. The company has successfully developed strong physical and intellectual assets, all while maintaining the energy, passion, and forward-thinking mindset needed to meet the demands of modern India.
